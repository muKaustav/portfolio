# Your Name

Hey, I'm Kaustav Mukhopadhyay, a final year CS undergrad at KIIT DU, Bhubaneswar. My passion lies in backend development, primarily with **_NodeJS_** and architecting scalable, robust and highly available systems. I use **_ReactJS_** for crafting captivating user experiences.

I have also been delving into the world of DevOps and System Design, and I'm currently learning **_Kubernetes_** and **_Docker_**. I'm also a huge fan of **_Golang_** and I'm currently learning the language. I also enjoy reading Alex Wu's blog posts on system design. I've built projects like a [Notification Service](https://github.com/muKaustav/notif-ease) and a [URL Shortener](https://github.com/muKaustav/ShortURL), among others.

I would call myself an amateur cinephile during my leisure time. I also find joy in reading books, and I've delved into a considerable collection of Murakami's literary works.
